## IBM Plex

The web UI bundles the IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono typefaces (via the
[Fontsource](https://fontsource.org) packages `@fontsource/ibm-plex-sans` and
`@fontsource/ibm-plex-mono`), so no font is fetched from a CDN at runtime.
